about the project

KERN is a concept racing ski brand created from scratch to compete in international design competitions. The goal is to convey through the interface what the brand puts into the product: precision, control and feeling of the track from the first turn.

my role

The only author of the project. Full cycle from idea to publication.

brand concept, philosophy and positioning, competitive analysis of the ski industry and digital references, copywriting, Midjourney content and 3D product visualization, website design, adaptive tools, UI Kit and transfer to assembly on TapTop

Brand character and mood board

I identified four key brand associations and translated each into a specific visual technique. Together they form the core of the KERN design system: speed, minimalism, precision and engineering.

From a wide field, I filtered out anchor images - those that most accurately convey the character of KERN: contrast, movement, engineering aesthetics.

Composition references

I decomposed each screen of the site separately: I selected compositional references for the specific task of the section - from the preloader to the footer.

ULTRA-UI Layers

ULTRA-UI is my own methodology: I break the design into layers - typography, grid, color, animation, graphics - and define which visual technique conveys the desired emotion for each one.

Product Design

Before modeling the ski, I collected visual references on the design: fastenings, bends, materials, detailing of joints. The focus is on products where the engineering form itself becomes a visual language.

Based on the references, I assembled a 3D model of the ski in Blender - with the exact geometry of the bindings, bend profile and structural details. Then I set up the textures, lighting and animation of the explosion diagram.

Let's take a look at the site itself now!

The camera starts with a close-up of a skier in goggles, moves toward the eye, passes through the pupil - and brings the viewer inside the KERN engineering system.
